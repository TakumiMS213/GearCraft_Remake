Sequence:
1. Wait for Title loading/camera to settle, retaining original selection. Capture baseline GAMESTART.
2. Raycast screen center of StartButton through the active EventSystem. Require the top hit is a GraphicRaycaster result belonging to that button. Send pointer-enter and check CanvasRenderer color equals native highlightedColor and differs from baseline, then capture.
3. Raycast SettingButton and send enter/down/up/click. Button.OnPointerClick invokes existing native onClick once. Capture open SettingPanel. Close through HideSetting, confirm hidden.
4. Restore Title's input module/navigation state BEFORE clicking StartButton so SceneTransitionManager can capture and block native navigation normally. Click GAMESTART through the same raycast/pointer path. Capture Story; do not call private story/page methods.
5. Wait native auto progression: OpeningStory 5 x (4+0.7) seconds, arrival 2 x roughly (4+camera/fade) seconds, camera restoration and scene fades. Both arrival flags must clear, camera must settle and FirstBedGuideArrow must be visible.
6. Capture two bed-arrow frames. Check first-day preparation and task text, visible sprite, left-center anchor, on-screen bounds and a measurable horizontal sway. Stop the callback in CraftSpace Play; do not sleep or advance the day.

Expected runtime: about 45–60 seconds; real-wall watchdog 150 seconds and per-PNG deadline 15 seconds. A stopped/paused Play aborts. Progress is ui-visual-progress.txt; each run uses a unique ui-visual-TIMESTAMP-GUID directory, never overwriting screenshots. ScreenCapture.CaptureScreenshot is asynchronous; the next action waits for a complete PNG IEND plus a 1920x1080 IHDR. No Camera.Render, CaptureScreenshotAsTexture, new RenderTexture, asset writes, private tutorial progress changes, direct scene loads or direct Button.onClick.Invoke are used.

This is EventSystem handler / render validation, not physical OS mouse or keyboard automation. Title InputModule is temporarily disabled to prevent the real cursor from overwriting synthetic hover; selection is retained, and input/navigation is restored on either success or failure. Later scenes retain their normal input. Do not interact with the running probe. Story / arrival still read actual mouse input, so external clicks would shorten their normal waits.

The existing fresh canonical 16-key backup already covers this; no legacy backup is imported.
